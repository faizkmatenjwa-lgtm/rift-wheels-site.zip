Frequently asked questions

Is insurance included in the rental price?

Yes. All vehicles carry comprehensive cover. An excess applies in the event of a claim and is stated in your rental agreement.

Can I drive outside Kenya?

Cross-border travel requires written approval and additional cover. Please contact us at least 3 days before your trip.

What is your cancellation policy?

Cancel free of charge up to 48 hours before pick-up. Later cancellations forfeit one day's rental.

Do you offer airport pick-up?

Yes. Our JKIA branch operates for all scheduled arrivals. Please share your flight number when booking.
